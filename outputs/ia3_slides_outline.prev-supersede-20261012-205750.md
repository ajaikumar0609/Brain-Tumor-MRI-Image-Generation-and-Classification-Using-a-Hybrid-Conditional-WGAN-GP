1. **Title** — Brain Tumor MRI Generation and Classification Using a Hybrid Conditional WGAN-GP. Name, course, date. Figure: `outputs/gan_comparison/sample_grids_real_vs_generated.png` (optional backdrop).
2. **Problem and questions** — Limited medical data; can GANs help? Q1: is cWGAN-GP a better generator than DCGAN/WGAN-GP? Q2: do its images help a classifier once training size is controlled? Academic study, no clinical claims.
3. **Dataset and preprocessing** — Kaggle brain-tumour MRI variant (exact source unverified; contains 'aug' files); 4 classes; Training 5,600 (1,400/class), Testing 1,600 (400/class); grayscale 64×64; 80/20 split (4,480/1,120). Testing used only for FID/KID reference and final evaluation (but it overlaps Training; see slide 16). Table: dataset summary.
4. **Models** — DCGAN, WGAN-GP, cWGAN-GP (50-d label embedding in G; projection critic D(x,y)=ψ(h(x))+⟨E(y),h(x)⟩). Table: architecture/optimizer summary.
5. **Objectives and training** — BCE vs Wasserstein + GP (λ=10, 5 critic steps); 3,000 generator updates; runtimes 204 s vs ~1,200 s. Table: training runs.
6. **Evaluation protocol** — FID and KID (torch-fidelity, pool3), 1,600 generated vs 1,600 real, 3 seeds, KID polynomial kernel and 100 subsets; real-vs-real reference.

11. **Leakage story** — Preliminary run: GAN saw validation images → optimistic; labelled preliminary, not used for conclusions. Leaky B 0.869 vs clean B 0.852, shown only to illustrate impact. Table: leaky vs clean.
12. **Downstream results (clean)** — Table A 0.811, B 0.852, C 0.869 (± sd, macro-F1); paired differences B−C mean −0.017. Per-class F1 table; glioma recall weakest.
13. **Interpretation** — B>A is matched by a size-matched control (C ≥ B in all seeds); no evidence of synthetic benefit; duplicates are not new data; C doesn't control diversity; no significance test.
14. **Limitations and reproducibility** — 3 seeds, single GAN run, short training, unverified synthetic labels, Metal non-finite-loss incident (cause unconfirmed, rerun reproduced results), 44 byte-duplicate validation images (3.9%) and, more broadly, train/test and train/validation near-duplicates (slide 16), Testing reused for FID/KID, C origin (external reviewer suggestion), process notes.
15. **Conclusion and future work** — DCGAN best FID/KID; cWGAN-GP second; no downstream benefit shown beyond size. Future: longer training, more seeds, label verification, significance tests. Conclusions are provisional: Testing overlaps Training (slide 16). References slide (arXiv refs checked; dataset source unverified): arXiv 1411.1784, 1511.06434, 1704.00028, 1706.08500, 1801.01401, 1802.05637; Kaggle dataset.
16. **Dataset audit: train/test near-duplicates (critical limitation)** — 1,076 of 1,600 Testing images (67%) have a near-identical, often mirrored, copy in Training (similarity ≥ 0.999; 99.8% same label; 250 sampled pairs checked at 128×128); exact SHA-256 found 0 because copies are not byte-identical; 627 of 1,120 CNN-validation images (56%) have a copy in CNN-training. Condition C test accuracy: 0.925 with a near-copy in Training (n=1,087) vs 0.750 without (n=513) vs 0.618 clearly different (n=273). Consequences: test accuracy, validation selection and the FID/KID reference are optimistic / not held-out; downstream conclusions provisional; next step: cluster-aware de-duplicated re-split, retrain GAN, rerun with predictions saved (~45-60 min).
